Caregiver – UI/UX Prototype

Caregiver is an interactive prototype designed in Figma to demonstrate my experience with user-centered design, 
interface planning, and the development of intuitive digital experiences. The project allowed me to translate an
application concept into a structured, navigable prototype while considering usability, accessibility, 
information organization and the overall user journey. This project demonstrates my ability to 
combine technical problem-solving with thoughtful interface design and practical application development.
